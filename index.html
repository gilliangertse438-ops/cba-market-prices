Methodology

Where every number comes from

  1. Compiled from public market reporting

    Every four days we gather the latest published figures from SMM, Mysteel, Fastmarkets market notes and producer announcements such as South32 and Samchrome.

  2. Checked before it is published

    Each figure is matched to its grade and delivery basis and tested against plausibility bounds. Moves of more than 35% between updates are held back for review.

  3. Converted at the rate of the day

    Rand values use the European Central Bank USD/ZAR reference rate for the date each price applies to, not today's rate.

  4. Traced to the mine gate

    Regional prices are netbacks: the CIF China benchmark less the logistics cost of each region's export route.

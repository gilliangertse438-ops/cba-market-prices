Methodology

How the prices are set

  1. Compiled by the CBA desk

    Prices are gathered from current market reporting and trade feedback, then reviewed and published by the ChromeBridge Africa desk.

  2. CIF, bulk, per metric ton

    Every price is a cost, insurance and freight (CIF) bulk rate per metric ton for chrome ore concentrate of the stated Cr₂O₃ grade.

  3. Rand at today's rate

    Prices are set in US dollars. Rand values use the latest European Central Bank USD/ZAR reference rate.

  4. Your costs, your price

    The calculator works back from CIF using the costs you enter, so the ground price reflects your own route and operation.
